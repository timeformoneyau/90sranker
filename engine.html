How recommendations work

Phase 1 — Your Taste: Every time you pick a winner, the engine learns which genres, tones, vibes, and eras you prefer.

Phase 2 — Scoring: Every movie you haven't voted on is scored against your taste profile.

Phase 3 — The Crowd: The engine finds similar voters and boosts movies they loved that you haven't seen yet.

With fewer than 10 votes, you'll see popular picks. Recommendations get more personal as your vote count grows.
