Your Movie Profile

Movies picked for you, based on how you vote. The more you vote, the smarter it gets.

How this works

Phase 1 — Your Taste: Every time you pick a winner, the engine learns which genres, tones, vibes, and eras you prefer. Winners earn positive signal; losers earn negative. Over time, a preference profile emerges.

Phase 2 — Scoring: Every movie you haven't voted on yet is scored against your taste profile. Movies that match more of your preferred attributes rank higher.

Phase 3 — The Crowd: The engine finds other voters with similar taste (based on overlapping winners) and boosts movies they loved that you haven't seen yet. This is lightweight collaborative filtering — no black-box ML.

With fewer than 10 votes, you'll see popular picks. The recommendations get more personal as your vote count grows.
